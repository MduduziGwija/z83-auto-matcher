## Full description
Applying for government jobs means filling in the same Z83 form and attaching the same CV again and again. Z83 Auto-Matcher does the repetitive part for you.

1. Add this week's Public Service Vacancy Circular (PDF) and your CV (PDF).
2. See every post, search and filter by department or salary level, and pick the ones you want.
3. Check your details once. The app fills in your Z83 from your CV and remembers your answers.
4. Get a ready-to-send PDF for each post (Z83 first, then your CV), named by reference number.
5. Tap one button to open your email app with the address, subject, message and PDF already filled in. You check it and press Send.

YOUR DOCUMENTS STAY ON YOUR PHONE
Everything is read and built on your device. Nothing is uploaded. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Works best with a CV saved as a PDF from Word, Google Docs or your phone (not a photo or scan).
• Reads many CV layouts, including different ways of writing languages, qualifications, dates and referees. If something is missed, you can type it once.
• The app uses simple rules, not AI, and can make mistakes. Always check the circular and your Z83 before sending.
• Z83 Auto-Matcher is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Department of Public Service and Administration or any government department.

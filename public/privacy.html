Privacy Policy

Z83 Auto-Matcher is a free app made by Mduduzi Gwija. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

The short version

Your documents and details never leave your phone. We do not collect, receive, store or sell your personal information.

What the app does with your information

When you choose a vacancy circular and your CV, the app reads them on your device. It uses what it finds (for example your name, ID number, qualifications, work experience and referees) to fill in the Z83 form and to build a PDF for each post you pick. All of this happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

What is saved on your device

So that you only type your details once, the app saves your answers (including the signature and initials you draw) inside the app on your device. You can remove them at any time with “Clear saved details” on the details screen, or by uninstalling the app.

Sending your application

The app never sends anything for you. When you tap the email button, it opens your own email app with the message and PDF prepared. You check it and press Send yourself. The email is then handled by your email provider under their privacy policy.

No tracking

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers. It does not need an internet connection to read your files or build your PDFs.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It only opens files you choose and shares files you ask it to share.

Children

The app is for adults applying for jobs and is not directed at children.

Accuracy

Matching and form-filling use simple rules, not artificial intelligence, and can be wrong. Always check the circular and your Z83 before you send them. This app is an independent tool and is not affiliated with the Department of Public Service and Administration or any government department.
